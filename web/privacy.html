pantryrun

Your data

pantryrun is being tested. Here's what it keeps about you, in plain words.

Who runs it

pantryrun is run by Andrea Sordano, in Estonia. Andrea decides what is kept and is responsible for it.

What pantryrun keeps

  • Your name, asked on your first visit.
  • A random ID your browser keeps, so your dinners stay yours. If you came with a personal tester link, the name in the link is your ID.
  • What you do in the app, with the time: the dinners and items you pick, the stores you look at, the answers you get and what you tap on them.
  • The store you usually shop at, if you tell us.
  • Dinners you save, with anything you write in them, and recipe links you paste. When you paste a recipe's text, the text isn't kept, only the ingredient lines pantryrun didn't recognise, so it can learn them.
  • Your email address, only if you sign up for the weekly plan. It's kept by MailerLite, the service that sends the email, not in pantryrun's own database. MailerLite also tells us whether you open the email and which links you click.

pantryrun doesn't ask for a password, your location, or payment details. It never sees your login at a store: the Rimi browser extension runs in your own browser.

Why

To learn whether pantryrun helps people decide where to shop and what to cook, and to make it better. And to keep your dinners for next time. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Where

The app runs on Render and the data is stored with Supabase, both in Frankfurt, in the EU. The weekly plan email is sent by MailerLite (UAB MailerLite, Lithuania, EU). To find prices, pantryrun asks the stores' websites (Selver, Rimi, Barbora); nothing about you is sent to them.

For how long

While pantryrun is being tested, and at most 12 months after your last visit. Then it's deleted.

Deleting your data

Ask here and everything kept under your ID is deleted within 30 days: your name, what you did in the app, your saved dinners and, if you signed up, your email address at MailerLite. To stop only the weekly plan, use the unsubscribe link in any of its emails.
